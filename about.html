About Dunder Mifflin

Dunder Mifflin is the paper company for people who believe offices are comedy clubs with worse lighting. We manufacture and distribute stationery with a sense of humour— products designed to function almost perfectly while keeping morale suspiciously high.

Founded in a breakroom in 2009, our philosophy is simple: if it doesn’t spark a smile, it’s not leaving the warehouse. Our legendary Slightly Bent A4 was an accident that became a bestseller. Once customers realised printers were the problem, not us, we knew we had a hit.

We’re also serious about not taking ourselves too seriously. Sustainability matters to us, so we recycle everything—paper, packaging, and occasionally old punchlines. Our teams operate on radical transparency: we clearly label products that may require “gentle persuasion” to fit the tray.

Dunder Mifflin is headquartered in Dublin, with remote branches in every kitchen table with a laptop. If you’ve ever laughed at a spreadsheet title, you’re already one of us.
